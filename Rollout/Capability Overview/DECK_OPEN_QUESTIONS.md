## 6. Known issues in the underlying data (found while doing this)

Not blocking the deck, but they affect any figure re-derived later.

1. **The chat cost log double-counts on recent chats.** In the three most recent conversations
   the `api_cost_*` column records a **running session total** rather than that turn's cost,
   so `cost_report.py:143` (which sums the column) over-reports. This is what made chat 14's
   wrap-up look like it cost €4.38 for writing one file — it actually cost about €0.94.
   Everything in the deck was recomputed from token counts instead, which is unaffected.
2. ~~**€3.91 per conversation is stale.**~~ **Fixed 2026-08-19.** The figure is **€4.44** (service
   ticket €6.23, lab question €3.73), recomputed from token counts. Updated in `PROJECT.md` and
   `Access-and-Feedback-Loop.md`; the two dated §8 decision entries keep €3.91 with a supersession
   pointer, because a decision log records what was believed at the time.
3. ~~**`PROJECT.md` contradicts itself on the case count.**~~ **Fixed 2026-08-19** — §2 now reads
   **51 write-ups** with the full category breakdown.
4. **Concurrent chats contaminate the measured window percentages.** You spotted this — it is
   real, and it is why the deck's plan figures come from the *fitted* calibration
   (`plan_calibration.json`), which works from per-turn token counts and is immune to it.
