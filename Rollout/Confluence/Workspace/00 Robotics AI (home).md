# Robotics AI

Robotics-AI is a **workspace that AI assistants work in** when they answer questions about Skalar's robots and robot software. It gives the assistant our instructions, our scripts and our history of solved cases, so it can find answers in the software quickly and reliably instead of digging through raw files.

## Pages

- **What Robotics-AI is**: a workspace an AI can use, and why it exists.
- **What's in the workspace**: the instructions, procedures, scripts and knowledge an assistant can use.
- **What you can ask it**: the kinds of questions, what happens and what comes out.
- **How it works**: one flowchart per kind of question.
- **Ground rules**: what it always does and never does.

The plan for a web application, where anyone can ask a question without installing anything, has its own section: **Web application plan**.
